Why an operating system

Every phone today is a box of sealed apps that can't see each other, sold by companies that need your attention. Blue starts clean.

One operating system that runs agents in the background, remembers what matters to you, and takes on the tasks you'd rather not do. Working today, on one device.

What it does for you

Six things no other phone does. Scroll.

Background agents

Hand off the tasks you dread.

Expense reports, comparison shopping, disputing a charge, finding that receipt. Say it once. Blue works on it while you get on with your day.

  • Runs several tasks at once
  • Works across your apps and accounts
  • Reports back when it's done

Notifications that matter

Only what matters gets through.

Blue reads every notification so you don't have to. Duplicates from three apps become one. Ads never surface. Nothing is hidden, you're just not harassed.

  • One alert per thing, not one per app
  • Sorted by what it means to you
  • Everything still there when you look

Infinite memory

Never explain yourself twice.

Blue knows who Dad is, which airport, and what your usual order is. Passwords and the sensitive stuff live in the Vault, end-to-end encrypted. Every memory is yours to see, edit or delete, and it moves with you to your next phone.

  • "Let Dad know we'll be there in 5"
  • "Get me a ride to the airport"
  • "Order my usual"

Proactive help

It notices before you do.

The bill that's due, the reply you owe, the thing you do every Friday. Blue spots the repetitive and the forgotten, then offers to take it on. One tap and it's handled.

Full voice access

Talk to your whole phone.

Have a normal conversation in Live mode, or just say what you need and Blue gets to work in the thread. Unlike a chatbot, it can do everything on the phone. One conversation, whichever way you're talking.

  • Acts on the phone, not just in chat
  • Switch between voice and text mid-task
  • Quiet room? Type instead

Smart dictation

Say it messy. Send it clean.

Blue Dictation is built into every keyboard. Ums, tangents and half-sentences come out as ready-to-send text, in your own style, in thirteen languages.

  • Learns the names it hears you say
  • Describe a word you can't remember and it uses it
  • Switch languages mid-sentence

Also in the box

The practical parts

  • Your apps

    The Google Play Store* for everything you already use, plus Blue's own Messages, Calendar, Mail, Notes, Clock and Contacts, built into the OS so Blue can act inside them.

  • Privacy & security

    Your memories are yours to read, edit and delete. Sensitive things stay in the Vault, end-to-end encrypted. How we handle your data.

  • Moving from iPhone

    Plug your iPhone into your Mac, choose what to bring, and Blue reviews the transfer with you: chats, mail, photos, apps, eSIM. Home by dinner.

*Play Store is installed after initial setup.
